Everything your shop needs

Designed for everyday retail — from the billing counter to end-of-day reports.

Billing Counter

Add products by name or barcode, apply discounts, collect cash or Khalti payments, and print invoices in seconds.

Inventory Management

Organise products by category, set stock quantities, and get low-stock alerts on the dashboard automatically.

Staff Management

Register cashiers, assign roles, and track which staff member processed each sale — without sharing your admin credentials.

Sales Reports

Filter transactions by date, search by customer or invoice, and view daily revenue against your set goal.

Barcode Scanning

Use your device camera or a USB scanner to add products to the cart instantly. Each product gets its own barcode.

Printable Invoices

Every sale generates a clean, print-ready invoice with your shop details, itemised bill, and payment method.
